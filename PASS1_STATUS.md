(1) Stacked joint estimation of b (target `abn_oc`) and delta_cc (target `cc_abn`) on ONE common sample (rows where both targets and the design are non-missing) and the SAME design X. Coefficients = the two separate OLS fits. Joint DK covariance: per-row scores [x*e1, x*e2] summed by date into (T, 2k), Bartlett long-run with the spec lag, and sandwich blockdiag(bread, bread).
(2) f = b/delta_cc. Delta-method SE: var f = V_bb/d^2 - 2 b V_bd/d^3 + b^2 V_dd/d^4. Fieller CI from (b - f d)^2 <= z^2 (V_bb - 2 f V_bd + f^2 V_dd); return bounded/unbounded/empty plus the denominator t-stat.
